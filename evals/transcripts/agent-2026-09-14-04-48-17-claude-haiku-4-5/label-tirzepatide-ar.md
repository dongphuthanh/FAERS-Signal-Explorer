Based on the tirzepatide label, the most common adverse reactions reported in clinical trials are **gastrointestinal reactions**. Here are the specific frequencies from the label:

## Common Adverse Reactions in Type 2 Diabetes (MOUNJARO)

From the pool of placebo-controlled trials with adult patients with Type 2 Diabetes, the most frequently reported adverse reactions were:

| Adverse Reaction | Placebo | MOUNJARO 5 mg | MOUNJARO 10 mg | MOUNJARO 15 mg |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nausea | 4% | 12% | 15% | 18% |
| Diarrhea | 9% | 12% | 13% | 17% |
| Decreased Appetite | 1% | 5% | 10% | 11% |
| Vomiting | 2% | 5% | 5% | 9% |
| Constipation | 1% | 6% | 6% | 7% |
| Dyspepsia | 3% | 8% | 8% | 5% |
| Abdominal Pain | 4% | 6% | 5% | 5% |

[MOUNJARO label, version 40, Adverse Reactions section]

## Common Adverse Reactions in Weight Reduction (ZEPBOUND)

In the ZEPBOUND trials for weight reduction, nausea and diarrhea were similarly the most common, along with fatigue and vomiting.

## Serious Adverse Reactions

The label also highlights serious adverse reactions including:
- Risk of thyroid C-cell tumors
- Severe gastrointestinal adverse reactions
- Acute pancreatitis
- Acute kidney injury due to volume depletion
- Acute gallbladder disease
- Hypersensitivity reactions
- Diabetic retinopathy complications
