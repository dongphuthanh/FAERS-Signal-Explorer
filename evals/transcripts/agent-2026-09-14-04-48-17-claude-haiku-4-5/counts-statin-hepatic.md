---

In patients aged 65 and over, statins have **6,528 serious cases with hepatic terms** reported to FAERS across quarters 2025q3–2026q2 (out of 14,364 total statin cases in this age group).

The most frequently reported hepatic events are:

| Term | Cases |
|------|-------|
| Hepatic cytolysis | 139 |
| Drug-induced liver injury | 98 |
| Liver injury | 85 |
| Liver function test increased | 73 |
| Hepatic enzyme increased | 64 |
| Liver disorder | 60 |
| Mixed liver injury | 52 |
| Hepatitis cholestatic | 47 |
| Hepatitis | 45 |
| Hepatic function abnormal | 41 |
| Hepatocellular injury | 40 |
| Hepatic steatosis | 35 |
| Hepatic failure | 34 |

An additional 23 hepatic terms with fewer than 40 cases each were also reported, including hepatic cirrhosis (17 cases), hepatorenal syndrome (14 cases), fulminant hepatitis (13 cases), and hepatic encephalopathy (5 cases).

These are counts of distinct FAERS cases, not rates or incidence figures. FAERS has no denominator.
