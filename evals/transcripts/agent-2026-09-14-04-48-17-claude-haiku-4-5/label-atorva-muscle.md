The atorvastatin label (LIPITOR, version 8, dated 2024-04-15) addresses muscle symptoms in detail:

**Warnings and Precautions (Section 5.1, Myopathy and Rhabdomyolysis):**

The label states that "LIPITOR may cause myopathy (muscle pain, tenderness, or weakness associated with elevated creatine kinase [CK]) and rhabdomyolysis." It specifies that "Muscle symptoms and CK elevations may resolve if LIPITOR is discontinued."

Patients are instructed to "promptly report any unexplained muscle pain, tenderness or weakness, particularly if accompanied by malaise or fever."

**Adverse Reactions (Section 6):**

The label lists under Musculoskeletal and Connective Tissue Disorders: rhabdomyolysis and myositis.

It also reports musculoskeletal system adverse reactions from clinical trials including "musculoskeletal pain, muscle fatigue" and "neck pain."

**Immune-Mediated Necrotizing Myopathy (Section 5.2):**

The label notes "rare reports of immune-mediated necrotizing myopathy (IMNM), an autoimmune myopathy, associated with statin use" characterized by proximal muscle weakness and elevated serum creatine kinase that persists despite discontinuing the drug.
