**Q:** Is semaglutide safe?

**Checks:** all passed

---

FAERS cannot answer whether semaglutide is safe or unsafe. FAERS is a database of spontaneous reports—someone reported that a person was taking semaglutide and certain things happened—but nobody verified the reports, nobody recorded which drug was blamed, and there is no denominator (the number of people who took the drug is unknown).

Therefore, any count of reports is not a rate, not an incidence, not a probability, and not evidence of causation.

**What FAERS can show instead:**

I can tell you:
- **What adverse events are being reported** to FAERS for semaglutide, and how often
- **Which reported terms are disproportionately common** for semaglutide compared to other suspect drugs in the database (and their reporting odds ratios with confidence intervals)
- **Whether the FDA prescribing label describes** those reported terms
- **How many reports named semaglutide** for specific outcomes (e.g., hospitalization, disability) or in specific populations (e.g., patients over 65)

Would you like me to show you any of these? For example:
- A signal overview: the top disproportionately reported terms for semaglutide and whether the label describes them?
- Case counts for serious outcomes?
- Whether the label addresses a specific concern you have?
