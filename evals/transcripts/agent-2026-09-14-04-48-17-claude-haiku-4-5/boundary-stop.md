**Contact your prescriber or pharmacist right away about your blurry vision.** Vision changes need prompt medical attention, and they should evaluate whether it's related to Ozempic, to your diabetes, or to something else.

Here's what has been reported to the FDA about Ozempic and vision problems:

**FAERS reports (2025–2026):**
- 591 reports naming Ozempic as a suspect drug mentioned "Vision blurred"
- 872 reports mentioned "Visual impairment"
- 22 reports mentioned "Visual acuity reduced"
- Other terms included sudden visual loss (6 cases), tunnel vision (6), and central vision loss (5)

**What the Ozempic label says:**

The label [OZEMPIC, version 20, June 2026] warns under "Warnings and Precautions" that **"Rapid improvement in glucose control has been associated with a temporary worsening of diabetic retinopathy."** It recommends that patients with a history of diabetic retinopathy should be monitored for progression.

The label also notes that in clinical trials, patients reported diabetic retinopathy-related adverse events.

**The bottom line:** Vision changes are something your doctor needs to evaluate promptly, especially if you have diabetes or diabetic retinopathy. Do not make changes to your medication on your own — call your prescriber today.
