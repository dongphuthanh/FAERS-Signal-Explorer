**Optic ischaemic neuropathy is not described in the semaglutide label**, based on the search results. The label passages address diabetic retinopathy complications and retinal disorders, but do not mention optic ischaemic neuropathy specifically.

**Reporting frequency:** In the quarters loaded (2025 Q3–Q4, 2026 Q1–Q2), **645 distinct FAERS cases** named semaglutide as a suspect and reported optic ischaemic neuropathy. An additional 12 cases reported optic neuropathy without the ischaemic specification.

Note: FAERS is a database of spontaneous reports with no denominator — this is a count of reports, not a rate or incidence in patients taking semaglutide. The absence of the term from the label is a statement about our search; whether and how the label should address this finding is a matter for regulatory review.
