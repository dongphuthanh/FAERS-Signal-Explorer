Based on the tirzepatide labels (MOUNJARO for type 2 diabetes and ZEPBOUND for weight reduction), the most common adverse reactions are:

## MOUNJARO (Type 2 Diabetes)
[MOUNJARO label, version 40, 2026-08-27, Adverse Reactions section]

Adverse reactions occurring in ≥5% of patients in the pool of placebo-controlled trials:

| Adverse Reaction | Placebo | MOUNJARO 5 mg | MOUNJARO 10 mg | MOUNJARO 15 mg |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nausea | 4% | 12% | 15% | 18% |
| Diarrhea | 9% | 12% | 13% | 17% |
| Decreased Appetite | 1% | 5% | 10% | 11% |
| Vomiting | 2% | 5% | 5% | 9% |
| Constipation | 1% | 6% | 6% | 7% |
| Dyspepsia | 3% | 8% | 8% | 5% |
| Abdominal Pain | 4% | 6% | 5% | 5% |

## ZEPBOUND (Weight Reduction)
[ZEPBOUND label, version 40, 2026-08-28, Adverse Reactions section]

Adverse reactions in ≥2% of patients (and greater than placebo) in the weight reduction trials:

The most common were gastrointestinal, including:
- **Nausea** (8–28% depending on dose)
- **Diarrhea** (8–23% depending on dose)
- **Vomiting** (2–13% depending on dose)
- **Constipation** (5–17% depending on dose)
- **Abdominal Pain** (5–10%)
- **Dyspepsia** (4–10%)

Also reported: Injection Site Reactions (6–8%), Fatigue (5–7%), Hypersensitivity Reactions (5%), Eructation (4–5%), and Hair Loss (4–5%).

Gastrointestinal adverse reactions predominated: 56% of ZEPBOUND-treated patients experienced GI adverse reactions versus 30% of placebo patients. The label notes that the majority of nausea, vomiting, and diarrhea occurred during dose escalation and decreased over time.
