## Serious infections across anti-TNF drugs

Across the anti-TNF class in Q2 2026, **8,701 distinct serious cases** named an anti-TNF drug as a suspect and reported an infection term. The anti-TNF class comprises 11,115 serious cases overall.

**Leading serious infection terms reported:**

| Term | Cases |
|------|-------|
| Lower respiratory tract infection | 448 |
| Infection (general) | 414 |
| *Helicobacter* infection | 369 |
| Wound infection | 292 |
| Urinary tract infection | 130 |
| Upper respiratory tract infection | 109 |
| Ear infection | 96 |
| *Clostridium difficile* infection | 72 |
| Viral infection | 41 |
| Respiratory tract infection | 38 |

Opportunistic and atypical infections also appear: 22 cases of CMV, 21 of *Aspergillus*, 9 of *Pneumocystis jirovecii*, 7 of *Mycobacterium avium* complex, 6 of device-related infection.

**What this means:** These are reports—cases where someone reported that a patient on an anti-TNF drug experienced a serious infection. FAERS has no denominator; these counts say nothing about the rate or probability of infection in treated patients. However, the breadth and frequency of serious infections reported across the class is consistent with the known immunosuppressive mechanism of TNF inhibition.
