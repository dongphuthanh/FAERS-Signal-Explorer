The atorvastatin (Lipitor) label describes muscle symptoms extensively in the Warnings and Precautions section:

**Main warnings about muscle symptoms:**

The label states that "LIPITOR may cause myopathy (muscle pain, tenderness, or weakness associated with elevated creatine kinase [CK]) and rhabdomyolysis" [LIPITOR, version 8, April 2024, Warnings and Precautions 5.1]. It instructs prescribers to "Inform patients of the risk of myopathy and rhabdomyolysis when starting or increasing the LIPITOR dosage. Instruct patients to promptly report any unexplained muscle pain, tenderness or weakness, particularly if accompanied by malaise or fever" [same section].

**Muscle-related adverse reactions listed:**

The Adverse Reactions section (6) lists:
- Rhabdomyolysis and myositis under Musculoskeletal and Connective Tissue Disorders
- Musculoskeletal pain, muscle fatigue, and neck pain under Musculoskeletal System

**Immune-mediated necrotizing myopathy:**

The label also describes rare reports of "immune-mediated necrotizing myopathy (IMNM), an autoimmune myopathy, associated with statin use," characterized by "proximal muscle weakness and elevated serum creatine kinase that persists despite discontinuation of statin treatment" [same, Section 5.2].

The label instructs to "Discontinue LIPITOR if markedly elevated CK levels occur or if myopathy is either diagnosed or suspected. Muscle symptoms and CK elevations may resolve if LIPITOR is discontinued" [Section 5.1].
