# counts-deaths

**Q:** How many deaths have been reported with dupilumab?

**Tool calls:**
- query_adverse_events {"drug":"dupilumab","mode":"counts","outcomes":["DE"]}

**Checks:** framing: "incidence"

---

**84 cases** have been reported to FAERS naming dupilumab as a suspect drug with a fatal outcome (DE = death).

This is a count of distinct FAERS cases, not a rate, incidence, or risk estimate. FAERS has no denominator: the number of people who took dupilumab is unknown. The presence of a report does not mean the drug caused the death—it only means someone reported that a person was taking dupilumab when they died, and an outcome code marked the case as fatal.

The data loaded is from 2026q2 (up to Q2 2026).
