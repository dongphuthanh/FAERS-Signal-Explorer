**Key findings:**

- **Cyclic vomiting syndrome** (81 cases, ROR 250.52) is the most strikingly disproportionate finding and is entirely absent from the label.

- **Optic ischaemic neuropathy** (196 cases, ROR 58.83) is reported disproportionately and not described in the label.

- Appetite-related terms (hunger, food craving, lack of satiety, hyperphagia, increased appetite paradoxically) dominate the report. Many of these are **efficacy complaints rather than adverse events** — they represent therapeutic failure rather than harm. The label mentions appetite suppression as intended mechanism in WEGOVY's clinical pharmacology section.

- **Gastric motility disorders** (gastric hypomotility, gastrointestinal hypomotility, abnormal GI sounds) represent a pattern not explicitly named in the label, though impaired gastric emptying is mentioned.

- **Injection site effects** (discharge, indentation, needle track marks) are reported in 285 cases combined but absent from the label.

The data show 16,486 distinct cases for semaglutide versus 422,395 reports of all other suspect drugs (2026 Q2). The analysis suppressed 25 administrative or device-related terms not shown here.
