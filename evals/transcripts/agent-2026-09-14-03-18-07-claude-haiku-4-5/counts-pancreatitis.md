# counts-pancreatitis

**Q:** How many semaglutide cases reported pancreatitis?

**Tool calls:**
- query_adverse_events {"drug":"semaglutide","mode":"counts","term_pattern":"pancreatitis"}

**Checks:** all passed

---

**156 cases** of semaglutide reports named pancreatitis as a reaction term (through 2026 Q2).

The data also includes:
- 36 cases of acute pancreatitis
- 15 cases of necrotising pancreatitis  
- 4 cases of obstructive pancreatitis
- 4 cases of chronic pancreatitis

These are counts of distinct FAERS cases naming semaglutide as a suspect drug. FAERS is a spontaneous reporting system with no denominator; these numbers are not rates and do not measure risk.
