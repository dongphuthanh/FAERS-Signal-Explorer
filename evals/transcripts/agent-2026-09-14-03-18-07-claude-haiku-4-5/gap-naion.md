**Optic ischaemic neuropathy is not described in the semaglutide label.**

The label discusses diabetic retinopathy and diabetic retinopathy complications, but contains no mention of optic ischaemic neuropathy specifically. The closest label content covers:

- [WEGOVY, version 19, Adverse Reactions]: "retinal disorders were reported by 6.9% of patients treated with WEGOVY injection (semaglutide 2.4 mg)... The majority of events were reported as diabetic retinopathy (4%) and non-proliferative retinopathy (0.7%)."
- [OZEMPIC, version 20, Warnings and Precautions]: "Rapid improvement in glucose control has been associated with a temporary worsening of diabetic retinopathy."

**Reporting frequency:**

196 cases of optic ischaemic neuropathy have been reported to FAERS naming semaglutide as a suspect drug (in Q2 2026). An additional 6 cases mention optic neuropathy without the ischaemic qualifier.

These are counts of distinct reports. FAERS has no denominator, so this cannot be expressed as a rate or risk in treated patients.
