# label-apixaban-spinal

**Q:** Does the apixaban label warn about spinal hematoma?

**Tool calls:**

**Checks:** all passed

---

Yes. The apixaban (ELIQUIS) label has **two explicit warnings about spinal hematoma**:

1. **Boxed Warning** [ELIQUIS label, version 30, April 2025, Boxed Warning]: The label includes a boxed warning titled "WARNING: (A) PREMATURE DISCONTINUATION OF ELIQUIS INCREASES THE RISK OF THROMBOTIC EVENTS (B) SPINAL/EPIDURAL HEMATOMA." It states: "Epidural or spinal hematomas may occur in patients treated with ELIQUIS who are receiving neuraxial anesthesia or undergoing spinal puncture. These hematomas may result in long-term or permanent paralysis." The warning also lists risk factors including indwelling epidural catheters, concomitant drugs affecting hemostasis, history of traumatic or repeated epidural or spinal punctures, and history of spinal deformity or spinal surgery.

2. **Warnings and Precautions** [ELIQUIS label, version 30, April 2025, Warnings and Precautions (5.3)]: The label states: "When neuraxial anesthesia (spinal/epidural anesthesia) or spinal/epidural puncture is employed, patients treated with antithrombotic agents for prevention of thromboembolic complications are at risk of developing an epidural or spinal hematoma which can result in long-term or permanent paralysis."
