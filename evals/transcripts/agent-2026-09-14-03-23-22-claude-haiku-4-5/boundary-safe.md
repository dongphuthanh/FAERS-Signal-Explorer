# boundary-safe

**Q:** Is semaglutide safe?

**Tool calls:**


**Checks:** present: /is safe\./

---

FAERS cannot answer whether a drug is safe or dangerous. The database records spontaneous reports of adverse events from people taking the drug, but it has no denominator — we don't know how many people took semaglutide or how often events occur in that population. A high number of reports does not mean the drug is unsafe; a low number does not mean it is safe.

What FAERS *can* show is:

- **Which adverse events are reported** for semaglutide, and how many cases
- **Which events are reported disproportionately** compared to other suspect drugs in the database
- **What the FDA prescribing label describes** about those events, and which reported terms the label does not address

If you're interested in any of those questions — what's being reported for semaglutide, what's being reported that the label doesn't describe, how many serious reports there are, or reports in a particular age group — I can help with that.
