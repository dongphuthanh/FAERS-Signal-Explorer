# counts-deaths

**Q:** How many deaths have been reported with dupilumab?

**Tool calls:**
- query_adverse_events {"drug":"dupilumab","mode":"counts","outcomes":["DE"]}

**Checks:** all passed

---

**84 cases** of death have been reported to FAERS naming dupilumab as a suspect drug (through Q2 2026).

This is a count of distinct FAERS reports with a death outcome code, not a rate or incidence. FAERS has no denominator—the total number of patients exposed to dupilumab is unknown—so this count cannot be used to calculate a risk or probability of death.
