# counts-statin-hepatic

**Q:** Serious hepatic events reported for statins in patients over 65.

**Tool calls:**
- query_adverse_events {"drug_class":"statin","mode":"counts","age_bracket":"65+","outcomes":["DE","LT","HO","DS","CA","RI","OT"],"term_pattern":"hepat|liver"}

**Checks:** all passed

---

Among patients aged 65 and over, there were **1,682 cases with serious outcomes** (death, life-threatening, hospitalization, disability, congenital anomaly, required intervention, or other serious outcome) reported to FAERS for statin drugs, of which 25 distinct hepatic and liver-related terms were identified. Data are from 2026q2.

**Top serious hepatic events reported in patients over 65:**

| Term | Cases |
|------|-------|
| Hepatic cytolysis | 38 |
| Hepatic failure | 26 |
| Drug-induced liver injury | 23 |
| Mixed liver injury | 23 |
| Liver injury | 22 |
| Hepatocellular injury | 20 |
| Acute hepatic failure | 16 |
| Hepatitis cholestatic | 15 |
| Hepatic ischaemia | 14 |
| Hepatorenal syndrome | 14 |

The remaining 15 terms included hepatic enzyme increased (13 cases), liver function test increased (13 cases), liver disorder (12 cases), and others with lower counts.

These are counts of distinct FAERS cases: they are not rates, incidence figures, or evidence of causation. FAERS contains no denominator—the number of patients over 65 taking statins is unknown.
