Based on the tirzepatide labels, the most common adverse reactions listed in clinical trials are:

**Most Common Adverse Reactions (≥5% in clinical trials):**

**Gastrointestinal (GI) reactions** dominated the adverse reaction profile:

- **Nausea**: 4–18% (placebo 4%, MOUNJARO 15 mg 18%; ZEPBOUND 15 mg 28%)
- **Diarrhea**: 9–23% (placebo 9%, MOUNJARO 15 mg 17%; ZEPBOUND 15 mg 23%)
- **Decreased Appetite**: 1–11% (placebo 1%, MOUNJARO 15 mg 11%)
- **Vomiting**: 2–13% (placebo 2%, MOUNJARO 15 mg 9%; ZEPBOUND 15 mg 13%)
- **Constipation**: 1–17% (placebo 1%, ZEPBOUND 5 mg 17%; MOUNJARO 15 mg 7%)
- **Dyspepsia**: 3–8% (placebo 3%, MOUNJARO 5 mg and 10 mg 8%; ZEPBOUND 5 mg 9%)
- **Abdominal Pain**: 4–10% (placebo 4%, ZEPBOUND 15 mg 10%)

**Other common reactions (≥5%)**:
- Injection site reactions: 6–8%
- Fatigue: 5–7%
- Hypersensitivity reactions: 5%
- Hair loss (alopecia): 4–5%
- Gastroesophageal reflux disease: 4–5%
- Eructation: 3–5%

[MOUNJARO and ZEPBOUND labels, Adverse Reactions section, version 40, dated 2026-08-27 and 2026-08-28]

The labels note that most GI adverse reactions occurred during dose escalation and decreased over time.
