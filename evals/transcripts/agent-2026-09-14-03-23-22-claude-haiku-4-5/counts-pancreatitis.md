**156 cases** of pancreatitis were reported to FAERS naming semaglutide as a suspect. An additional 36 cases reported acute pancreatitis, 15 necrotising pancreatitis, 4 obstructive pancreatitis, and 4 chronic pancreatitis, for a total of 215 cases across pancreatitis-related terms.

(The data loaded is through Q2 2026. These are counts of distinct FAERS cases; no denominator is available, so these are not rates or risks.)
