The atorvastatin label (Lipitor, version 8, 2024-04-15) addresses muscle symptoms in detail:

**Warnings and Precautions (Section 5.1: Myopathy and Rhabdomyolysis):**

The label states that "LIPITOR may cause myopathy (muscle pain, tenderness, or weakness associated with elevated creatine kinase [CK]) and rhabdomyolysis." It also notes that "Acute kidney injury secondary to myoglobinuria and rare fatalities have occurred as a result of rhabdomyolysis in patients treated with statins, including LIPITOR."

The label identifies risk factors including age 65 years or older, uncontrolled hypothyroidism, renal impairment, concomitant use with certain other drugs, and higher LIPITOR dosage.

The label instructs: "Instruct patients to promptly report any unexplained muscle pain, tenderness or weakness, particularly if accompanied by malaise or fever." It also states that "Muscle symptoms and CK elevations may resolve if LIPITOR is discontinued."

**Warnings and Precautions (Section 5.2: Immune-Mediated Necrotizing Myopathy):**

The label describes rare reports of immune-mediated necrotizing myopathy (IMNM) associated with statin use, characterized by "proximal muscle weakness and elevated serum creatine kinase that persists despite discontinuation of statin treatment" along with positive antibody testing and necrotizing myopathy on muscle biopsy. It notes that "Treatment with immunosuppressive agents may be required" and instructs to "Discontinue LIPITOR if IMNM is suspected."

**Adverse Reactions (Section 6):**

The label lists musculoskeletal adverse reactions including "musculoskeletal pain, muscle fatigue" and serious reactions such as "rhabdomyolysis" and "myositis."
