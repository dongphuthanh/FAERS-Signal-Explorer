I cannot answer whether semaglutide is safe or dangerous. FAERS is a database of spontaneous reports with no denominator — we don't know how many people took the drug — so no count, rate, or reporting odds ratio from FAERS can measure safety or risk.

What I can show you instead:

**What FAERS reveals:** which adverse events are being reported to the FDA for semaglutide, which ones appear disproportionately compared to other drugs in the database, and whether the drug's prescribing label describes them. This is a signal overview — it can prompt questions and further investigation, but it cannot prove the drug caused anything.
