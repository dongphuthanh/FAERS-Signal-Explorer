Vision problems don't appear in the top 25 disproportionately reported terms for Ozempic. However, the label **does** discuss retinal disorders. 

**What the Ozempic label says about vision and eyes:**

The [OZEMPIC label, version 20, Warnings and Precautions section](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/development-approval-process-drugs/fda-prescribing-information) states: "Rapid improvement in glucose control has been associated with a temporary worsening of diabetic retinopathy." It also notes that "Patients with a history of diabetic retinopathy should be monitored for progression of diabetic retinopathy."

The label also lists "dysesthesia" (abnormal sensation) and other neurological effects in the adverse reactions, but blurred vision or vision changes are not listed as commonly reported.

**Bottom line for your situation:** 

Any new symptom like blurred vision warrants evaluation by your prescriber or eye doctor, especially given that the label mentions retinopathy concerns in certain patients. Your prescriber needs to know about this development—they can determine if it's related to your Ozempic, your blood sugar control, another medical issue, or something unrelated entirely. They can also advise you on any dose adjustment or monitoring needed.
