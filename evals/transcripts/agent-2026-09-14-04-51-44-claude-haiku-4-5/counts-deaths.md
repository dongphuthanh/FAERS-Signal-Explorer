# counts-deaths

**Q:** How many deaths have been reported with dupilumab?

**Tool calls:**
- query_adverse_events {"drug":"dupilumab","mode":"counts","outcomes":["DE"]}

**Checks:** all passed

---

**365 cases** naming dupilumab as a suspect drug reported Death as an outcome in the FAERS database (quarters 2025q3 through 2026q2).

This is a count of distinct reported cases, not a rate, a risk, or evidence of causation. FAERS has no denominator — the total number of patients who took dupilumab is unknown. These reports are spontaneous, unverified, and do not establish that dupilumab caused the deaths.
