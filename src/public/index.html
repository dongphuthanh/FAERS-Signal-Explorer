  • Follow-up questions work: after an answer, try “and for tirzepatide?” or “only in patients under 45”.
  • If a drug “isn't in the database”, check the spelling first — Janumet, not Janumed.
  • Answers take 10–40 seconds; the line under your question shows what is being looked up.

FAERS is a spontaneous reporting system with no denominator. Every number here is a count of reports or a ratio of reporting proportions — never an incidence, a risk, or evidence of causation. “No matching label text” describes our search of the label versions loaded, nothing more.
